HELP CENTER

Frequently asked questions

Can I withdraw my LP before 180 days?

No. The deployed locker contract requires the position to reach its unlock timestamp before LP withdrawal.

Are LP trading fees paid as a separate claim?

The locker does not expose a separate LP-fee harvesting function. Trading fees accrue in the PancakeSwap V2 pair reserves and can affect the value of the underlying LP position. The actual withdrawal amount depends on pool conditions.

How are RND rewards calculated?

The contract values the actual RND used through its TWAP oracle, compares it with the actual USDT used, and awards 20 RND for each full $50 matched step.

Can I add a referral later?

Your referrer is fixed when your wallet is first registered. If your wallet was first registered without a referrer, the contract does not allow attaching one later.

When can rewards be claimed?

Rewards mature at the same unlock timestamp as the associated LP position. Claims also require enough RND in the locker reserve for that position's payout.

Does the website hold my funds?

No. Transactions are signed by your wallet and executed by the smart contract. Never share your seed phrase or private key with anyone.
